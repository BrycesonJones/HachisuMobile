Hachisu

Bitcoin payments for merchants.

Hachisu is a non-custodial Bitcoin payment app for merchants and small businesses. Accept Bitcoin from your customers, invoice them, and run a point of sale — while every payment settles directly to a Bitcoin wallet only you control.

What Hachisu does

Accept Bitcoin payments

Take Bitcoin from customers in person or online, and watch payments settle in your store activity.

Create invoices

Bill a customer for a specific amount and share a payment link they can settle from any Bitcoin wallet.

Send payment requests

Issue a reusable request for a fixed or open amount, and track whether it has been paid.

Run a point of sale

Ring up items from a product list, or charge a one-off amount without building a cart.

Add a pay button

Put a Bitcoin payment button in front of customers without rebuilding your existing checkout.

Track store activity

Review payments as they arrive, open any transaction in detail, and export monthly statements.

Custody

Hachisu is non-custodial. You connect your own Bitcoin wallet, and customer payments settle directly to it. Hachisu never holds, controls, or transmits your Bitcoin, and never has access to your private keys or your recovery phrase.

Because Hachisu does not custody funds, closing your account does not put your Bitcoin at risk — your wallet is yours, and it keeps working whether or not you use Hachisu.

Who it is for

Hachisu is built for merchants and small businesses that want to accept Bitcoin without handing custody of it to a payment processor. It is a merchant tool, not a consumer wallet: it is designed around taking payment for goods and services, keeping a record of what was paid, and settling to infrastructure the business already controls.

What Hachisu supports today

Currency
Bitcoin only. Hachisu is not a general cryptocurrency payment platform.
Settlement
On-chain Bitcoin.
Lightning
Lightning is not currently available to merchants in Hachisu.
Custody
Non-custodial. Payments settle to a wallet you connect and control.
Platform
A mobile app for iPhone.
Availability
In development. Hachisu is not yet released on the App Store.
