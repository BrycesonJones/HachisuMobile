Hachisu is a non-custodial Bitcoin system for people, businesses, and communities building independent economies around money they control. Accept Bitcoin from your community in person or online, create invoices, and run a point of sale. Every transaction settles directly to a Bitcoin wallet only you control. Hachisu never holds your Bitcoin, and never has access to your private keys or your recovery phrase.
